Read Before You Snap

RULES & REGULATIONS

"With great power comes great responsibility." Every hero plays by the rules — here's the code of conduct for Genesis Snap.

🏛️

Venue & Logistics

  • Punctuality is mandatory — arrive at the venue (IEM New Town / UEM) by 9:00 AM sharp. Registration and check-in will wrap up quickly, and the event officially commences at exactly 9:30 AM.
  • College ID is mandatory — every student must carry their physical college ID card. It will be verified at the registration desk, and entry will be denied without it.
  • Professional dress code — formal, decent attire is mandatory for all participants for the entire duration of the event.
🤖

The "Gemini-Only" AI Protocol

  • Google Gemini is the only AI platform permitted during this hackathon. The use of any other AI tools or code assistants is strictly prohibited.
  • Mandatory Prompt Audit Log — every team must submit a single PDF alongside their final project, containing screenshots, shared links, or a full export of every conversation and prompt used in Gemini during the event.
  • If the audit log is missing or incomplete, the project will not be evaluated.
💻

Bring Your Own Tech

  • Participants are responsible for bringing their own laptops, chargers, and any necessary adapters.
  • Come prepared — plan for your own hardware for the full 8-hour hackathon.

Still Have Questions?

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything a rookie hero needs to know before the snap.

Who can participate in Genesis Snap?

Genesis Snap is open to all college students across India. Teams must have 2–4 members — solo entries aren't accepted.

What should I bring to the venue?

Your laptop, charger, and any adapters you need, plus your physical college ID card — entry is not allowed without it. Formal, decent attire is mandatory too.

Will food be provided?

Yes! Meals, snacks, and unlimited coffee/tea are provided throughout the 8-hour hackathon so you can stay focused on building.

Which AI tools are we allowed to use?

Only Google Gemini is permitted during the hackathon — no other AI tools or code assistants are allowed. See the Rules & Regulations section above for the full protocol, including the mandatory Prompt Audit Log.

What happens after Round 1?

A jury reviews every project submitted during the hackathon. The top 20 teams are then announced and advance to Round 2, the live pitching round.

How does the pitching round work?

Each of the top 20 teams gets 3 minutes to pitch, followed by 4 minutes of Q&A with the judging panel, in front of a live audience.

Can we use any tech stack?

Yes — build with whatever tech stack your team is comfortable with. The only constraint is that your project must be built from scratch during the hackathon, using Gemini as your only AI assistant.

Is there a registration fee?

Registration details, including any fee, will be shared on the official registration page — check back closer to the event.
